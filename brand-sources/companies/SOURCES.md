# Company logo sources

These files are the companies' own logos, downloaded unmodified from their official websites on 2026-10-26.
They are not redrawn, recoloured or cropped. The originals are kept here; the site serves resized WebP
copies (160px tall, same artwork) from `public/images/companies/`. On the site they sit on a white plate in
both themes so the original colours stay legible.

| File | Company | Source page | Direct file URL |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pat-iot-solutions.png` (332×220, transparent PNG) | PAT IoT Solutions | https://patiot.in/ | https://patiot.in/images/logo.png |
| `csr-electronologies.jpg` (575×576, JPEG, white background) | CSR Electronologies | https://www.csre.in/ | https://www.csre.in/images/logo.jpeg |

Note: csre.in lists the company's address as Hanamkonda, Telangana. The résumé lists the internship location as Hyderabad.

To replace a logo, overwrite the file (or add a new one) and update `logo` in `src/content/experience.ts`.
